Grad School Application Resources

No one person has a complete view of graduate admissions: decisions are made by individual professors or admissions committees at individual departments. The best anyone can offer is direct personal experience, things they heard, and common sense.

Shriram Krishnamurthi gives a detailed, frank, and incisive look at the PhD application process from the faculty side. There is also a detailed and more up-to-date written FAQ.

A document aimed at CMU students about applying to grad school. Note that the document is from 2014; CS has become more competitive since then.
